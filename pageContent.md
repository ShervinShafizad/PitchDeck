# pitch deck

## Game summary: 
A 2D, single player, offline mobile game about a thief in a fictional city set in 1960s era. The 
game has missions based on the principle of stories. Each mission is a new story. 

## Design Pillars: 
Combat (shootout between criminals). Story telling (using elements such as comic style panels).  
Purchasing ability (using in game currency to buy items). 

## Mechanics: 
The game uses unique mechanics for aiming, switching weapons, changing camera view, and 
other actions. 

## Features: 
Progression elements for weapons and missions. Auto save and persistency. Different enemy 
types. 

## Interface:  
Controlling the main character using device acceleration and UI buttons for interaction, moving, 
aiming, shooting, switching weapons, etc.  

## Art Style:  
Monochrome 2D art style where the environment is black and white. Intractable items will have 
distinctive color.  

## Music/Sound:  
Atmospheric piano melodies for music during story sequences. Electronic rock beats for 
intense combat parts. Industrial ambient sounds for calm moments. Voice lines may or may not be implemented. 

## Development period:
one year. 

## Core team size: 
2 people, a programmer and an artist. 

## Audience:
Above 12 years of age. People with a passion for action shooting games. 

## Platform: 
android / iOS 

## Monetization strategy:
a free to play game with in app purchases but no adds.
